## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

I interpret user needs by identifying what customers want from the software and translate that into specific requirements. User stories help with this by providing the team with a clear description of what the user needs and why, while acceptance criteria provides specific conditions that can be reflected on to see if the requirement has been met. 
 
## How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing a program by first understanding the requirements and then breaking them down into smaller, easier to digest, pieces. In my future development work, I hope to incorporate Agile processes such as user stories, regular testing, feedback, and retrospectives so that I am able to adapt to changing requirements and deliver the best product possible. 

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development means communicating clearly, completing assigned work, helping others if asked, and being open minded to feedback. A good team member should also be willing to keep the teams overall goals in mind rather than focusing only on their individual responsibilities.
